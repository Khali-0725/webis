## Holiday calendar

`backend/config/holidays.php` contains 2026 and 2027 national regular and special non-working dates. It excludes the special **working** EDSA day. The 2026 list follows [Proclamation 1006](https://pco.gov.ph/news_releases/pbbm-issues-proclamation-declaring-regular-holidays-special-non-working-days-for-2026/) plus the later [Eid'l Fitr](https://pco.gov.ph/news_releases/proclamation-no-1189-s-2026-declaring-friday-20-march-2026-a-regular-holiday-throughout-the-country-in-observance-of-eidl-fitr-feast-of-ramadhan/) and [Eid'l Adha](https://pco.gov.ph/news_releases/palace-declares-may-27-as-a-regular-holiday-for-eidl-adha-observance/) proclamations. The 2027 list follows [Proclamation 1427](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/7/102791). Add later 2027 Eid proclamations and future-year proclamations when issued. Local-only holidays are not in this national calendar. The toggle does not cancel existing bookings.

## Manual role walkthrough

Use disposable local accounts and synthetic files only. Start Laragon MySQL, the Laravel server in `backend`, and Vite in `frontend` as described in `HOW-TO-RUN.md`.

1. **Provider:** Register a new provider; confirm business routes redirect to verification. Upload an ID and permit, check both show pending, and verify that service publishing and new bookings are blocked. After admin approval, publish a service. Replace one required document and confirm the provider returns to pending until the replacement and final decision are approved. Test the optional document separately; it should not revoke approved status.
2. **Administrator:** Open Verifications. Confirm an account with no uploads appears under Unverified. Preview each private file, approve the ID only, and confirm final Verify provider is still blocked. Approve the permit and complete final approval. Separately try bulk approve/decline with eligible and already reviewed documents; rejection requires a reason. Check audit history. Review a report, review archive/restore, suspension, and appeal decision using test cases.
3. **Client:** Search by municipality/category, open a verified provider, choose an available slot, place a location pin, and submit a booking. Confirm the provider sees only the authorized booking details. After acceptance, upload an exact-amount synthetic payment proof and reference; under/overpayment must be rejected. Confirm the proof stays unverified until provider/admin review. Open, download, and print the receipt; compare its label and values with the payment record. Complete the booking, submit one review, and confirm edit/delete are unavailable. File a test report and inspect only the allowed status updates.
4. **All roles and viewport sizes:** Confirm logout cancellation leaves the session active; confirmation ends it. Check notifications, back-to-dashboard navigation, map containment, dialogs, and form controls at 320, 375, 768, and 1366 CSS pixels. For session policy, use two independent browser contexts and a controlled clock/test environment rather than waiting 30 real minutes.
